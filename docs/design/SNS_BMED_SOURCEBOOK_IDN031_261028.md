### 1.1 Selective photothermolysis — 레이저 피부 시술의 기원과 주의사항

상태: USE_WITH_CAUTION

공식 URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6836297/

검증 범위:
- Anderson RR, Parrish JA. "Selective photothermolysis: precise microsurgery by selective absorption of pulsed radiation." Science 1983;220(4596):524-527. (Harvard Medical School, Massachusetts General Hospital 피부과)
- 초록: 충분히 짧은 펄스로 선택적으로 흡수되는 빛은 살아 있는 조직에서 색소 구조물·세포·세포소기관을 선택적으로 손상시킬 수 있고, 정밀한 조준이 필요 없다.
- 실험 사례: 577nm 펄스로 피부 미세혈관, 351nm 펄스로 멜라닌세포 안의 멜라노솜에 선택적 손상이 나타났다.

SNS 콘텐츠 핵심 메시지:
Laser skin treatment traces back to selective photothermolysis, described in a 1983 Science paper by Harvard Medical School researchers. The idea: light that a target structure absorbs, delivered in very short pulses, concentrates heat damage on that structure. The paper shows a principle, not a guaranteed treatment result, so precautions (주의사항) should be confirmed with a doctor.

주의:
Do not say this paper is the origin of every laser treatment. Do not state or imply results, safety, or guarantees. No device settings or how-to steps.

이미지 개념: abstract diagram of a light pulse being absorbed by a small target inside skin layers, calm clinical blue tones
